# PNI 2026 — DTP routine rule verification

## Scope

Python-only routine childhood DTP evaluator.

The evaluator consumes only the locked normalized DTP child-history model
`PNI26-DTP-CHILD-HISTORY-001`.

It does not reinterpret raw vaccine histories.

## Routine window

The ordinary DTP childhood layer begins at exact 15 months and closes at
the exact seventh birthday.

No DTP administration is generated by this rule at or after exact age 7.

## Prerequisite

Routine DTP boosters require the qualifying three-dose pentavalent basic
series preserved by the locked history normalizer.

Aggregate diphtheria/tetanus/pertussis antigen counts are not accepted as
a substitute.

If that pentavalent prerequisite is not complete, this DTP rule does not
invent or implement the missing pentavalent schedule.

## R1

The first DTP booster is due only after both conditions are satisfied:

- age at least 15 calendar months; and
- at least six calendar months after qualifying pentavalent D3.

The authoritative threshold is therefore:

`max(exact15m, qualifying D3 + 6 calendar months)`.

## R2

The second DTP booster is due only after both conditions are satisfied:

- age at least 4 calendar years; and
- at least six calendar months after valid R1.

The authoritative threshold is:

`max(exact4y, valid R1 + 6 calendar months)`.

## Calendar-month contract

The six-month rules are calendar-month intervals.

They are not converted to 180, 181, 182, 183 or another fixed number of
days.

The exact calculated `recommended_date` carries timing authority.

## Age-7 closure

The six-month minimum is never shortened to force R2 into the DTP age
window.

If a valid late R1 produces an earliest R2 date on or after the exact
seventh birthday:

- no R2 DTP recommendation is produced;
- the DTP opportunity is considered closed;
- this rule does not synthesize the later dT schedule.

## Context gating

Special-condition and administration-safety context is requested only
when a DTP dose would otherwise be due.

A non-due or completed schedule does not demand unnecessary context.

Explicit special-condition or safety concern routes to review rather than
automatic whole-cell DTP administration.

## Deferred pathways

This routine core does not automate:

- neurologic or serious pertussis-component adverse-event pathways;
- CRIE substitutions;
- outbreak/contact-control immunoprophylaxis;
- pentavalent substitution because DTP is unavailable;
- future dT scheduling.

## Interaction boundary

No live-vaccine interaction schema is introduced.

Routine DTP has no dedicated inter-vaccine spacing layer in this model.

## Localization and safety

PT-BR is primary and EN-GB is a complete secondary interpretation.

Decision logic is language-neutral.

No special condition is inferred.

No synthetic vaccination score is produced.
