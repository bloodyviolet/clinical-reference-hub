# NEWS2 — Brazilian adaptation provenance

Clinical review: 2026-09-11

## International reference

The numerical NEWS2 implementation remains based on the Royal College
of Physicians NEWS2.

No Brazilian numerical override is applied.

## Brazilian adaptation

Peer-reviewed source:

National Early Warning Score 2: transcultural adaptation to Brazilian
Portuguese.

Revista Gaúcha de Enfermagem. 2020;41.

DOI:
10.1590/1983-1447.2020.20190424

Source:
https://www.scielo.br/j/rgenf/a/Wc46R8wyJdLrGyffZQQQCcw/?lang=pt

The publication reports that:

- the Royal College of Physicians authorised the Brazilian
  cross-cultural adaptation before the process began;
- the adaptation used semantic, idiomatic, cultural and conceptual
  equivalence review;
- the expert committee included nurses and physicians from nine
  Brazilian states;
- mean Content Validity Index was 0.98;
- inter-rater agreement was perfect/almost perfect;
- the final adaptation was returned to the RCP and the publication
  reports RCP acceptance/approval of the final version.

## Scope implemented by Clinical Reference Hub

The application now uses terminology aligned with the validated
Brazilian adaptation for core NEWS2 interface terminology.

This does not mean every explanatory sentence generated by the
application is part of the published validated instrument.

Longer explanatory and implementation text remains locally authored and
is labelled accordingly in metadata.

## Numerical parity

The Brazilian adaptation does not change:

- physiological scoring thresholds;
- SpO2 Scale 1 or Scale 2 scoring;
- supplemental-oxygen score;
- aggregate NEWS2 total;
- single-red-parameter trigger;
- NEWS2 escalation bands.

Python and browser numerical-vector regression tests remain the
authoritative parity gate.

## National SUS status

The 2026 Brazilian harmonization audit did not identify a federal
Ministry/SUS instrument that replaces NEWS2 scoring nationally.

Institutional use in Brazil must not be described as a national SUS
mandate unless a current authoritative federal source establishes that.

## RCP translated-material gate

The current RCP NEWS2 resource page continues to specify conditions for
translated material.

Clinical Reference Hub therefore retains:

- translation_disclaimer_required = true;
- the original English NEWS2 as canonical;
- the existing final-release translation-compliance gate in
  `docs/V2_LANGUAGE_POLICY.md`.

Brazilian validation and the RCP translated-material compliance gate
are treated as separate requirements.
