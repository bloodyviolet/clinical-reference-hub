## Interpretation

A locator indicates that the source section supporting the summarized directive was identified; it does not turn the dashboard sentence into a verbatim quotation or replace professional interpretation of the full policy. `policy_context` rows are deliberately kept distinct so the application does not imply that diabetes, hypertension, G6PD deficiency, or the combined tobacco/alcohol summary are themselves standalone named directives of the cited base policy.

The current legal-interruption row was rewritten to avoid presenting a single procedure as universally required and now reflects current Ministry guidance on access to legally permitted interruption and sexual-violence health services.
